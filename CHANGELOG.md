## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-25

### Added
- Initial release of the Community Resilience Guidebook
- Part I: Foundations (core philosophy, the one-question test, acting on behavior not intent)
- Part II: The Moderation Charter (removal tiers, rules of engagement, handling pushback, self-check questions)
- Part III: Technical Security Controls (platform configuration, verification and onboarding, behavioral monitoring)
- Part IV: Recognizing Disruption Patterns (bad-faith conversation tactics, movement disruption patterns, social engineering, the four horsemen of movement collapse)
- Part V: Structural Defenses (tiered trust, decision-making, founder protection, compartmentalization)
- Part VI: Quick Reference (red flag checklist, pattern-to-response card, message templates)
- Appendix A: Adapting This Guidebook
- Appendix B: Prior Art and Further Reading
